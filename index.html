The Sanctuary

A quiet floor above the city

Climb one flight above the bustle of Green Park Market and the city softens. Candle-light moves across textured plaster, warm oil scents the air, and an arched corridor leads to suites designed to be disappeared into — for an hour, or an afternoon.

Every ritual happens behind its own door, on fresh single-use linens, with therapists who treat pressure as a conversation rather than a routine. Comfort, privacy and hygiene are not amenities here; they are the architecture.

PrivacyEvery treatment in a private, lockable suite.
HygieneSingle-use linens, sanitised after every guest.
ExpertiseTrained, certified and genuinely attentive therapists.
CalmUnhurried sessions — your time is never rushed.
